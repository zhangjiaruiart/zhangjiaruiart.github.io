Coral Planting Project

Documentary / 2025 / Xisha Islands

The Coral Planting Project is a visual documentation of ecological restoration filmed in the Xisha Islands in 2025. This project captures the delicate process of "reforesting" the seabed, focusing on the symbiotic relationship between human intervention and marine regeneration. Through the lens, the act of planting coral fragments becomes a meditative performance—a slow, silent commitment to the revival of a fragile ecosystem.

Unlike traditional nature documentaries, this work emphasizes the rhythmic silence of the underwater world and the technical precision of the restoration team. It explores the concept of "assisted recovery," where the boundary between the artificial and the natural blurs. Each coral seedling fixed to the nursery frames represents a point of hope in a vast, threatened landscape, reflecting the urgent need for a new ecological consciousness.

By recording the growth and structural complexity of the coral colonies, the film serves as both a scientific record and an artistic contemplation on time and survival. It invites the viewer to witness the invisible labor performed beneath the surface, fostering a deep sensory connection with the ocean’s enduring yet vulnerable life force.
