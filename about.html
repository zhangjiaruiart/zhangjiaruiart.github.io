Jerry

b. 1990

Zhang Jiarui's artistic creation has always revolved around two intertwined paths: one is "the contemporary transformation of traditional art," and the other is "the on-site practice of ecological art." These two paths are relatively independent directions of exploration, yet they form an intertextuality in creative logic and methodology, becoming an important fulcrum for my continuous art research.

His works are both a regeneration experiment of traditional Chinese aesthetics and an intervention in the contemporary ecological context. At a deeper level, both point to the same question: how to make art a bridge connecting tradition and the future, humanity and nature, and sensibility and ethics in the face of global ecological crisis and cultural rupture.
