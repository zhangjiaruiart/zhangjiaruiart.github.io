Zhang Jiarui

b. 1990, China

Zhang Jiarui's artistic creation revolves around two intertwined paths: "the contemporary transformation of traditional art" and "the on-site practice of ecological art." These two paths form a profound intertextuality in both creative logic and methodology.

His works represent both a regenerative experiment of traditional aesthetics and a critical intervention in the contemporary ecological context. They address a fundamental inquiry: how art can function as a bridge connecting tradition with the future amidst global ecological crises.
