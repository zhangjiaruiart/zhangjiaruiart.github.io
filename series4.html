Formless Series

I think the most attractive line in traditional Chinese modeling language is "curve". Religious sculptures since the Northern Dynasties have been ordered by lines in their volume, including the density of the lines, the shadows of the lines, the rhythm of the lines, and the tightness of the lines. The names and stories of this type of sculpture are the starting point for my creations.
