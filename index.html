Artist Statement

               

Zhang Jiarui is a contemporary artist based in China, whose practice spans sculpture, installation, and performance art. His work is deeply rooted in the concept of "Ecological Ethics," exploring how human intervention reshapes the natural landscape.

               

By using materials sourced directly from the environment and executing site-specific projects in remote areas—from the inner Mongolian plains to the coral reefs of the South China Sea—his work serves as a bridge between industrial civilization and ecological preservation. He believes that art is not just a visual object, but a meditative process that prompts viewers to reconsider their footprint on Earth.
